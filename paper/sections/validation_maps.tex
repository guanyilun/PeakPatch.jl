We compare angular power spectra against the released Websky v0.0 maps
(Nside 4096) on matched octant footprints, using gnomonic-cap flat-sky
spectra (cap-to-cap scatter as the error bar) and whole-octant pseudo-$C_\ell$
with an apodized mask. The simulations are different realizations of the same
configuration, so ratios consistent with unity at the realization scatter are
the pass criterion.
