\section{Map making}
\label{sec:maps}

Secondary-anisotropy maps combine two ingredients, mirroring the Websky
construction \citep{Stein2020}: \emph{field} maps, line-of-sight integrals
painted from every lattice cell of the displaced matter field, and
\emph{halo} maps, profile painting at catalogue positions. Field maps carry
the large-scale (two-halo and unresolved) signal; halo maps carry the
small-scale one-halo signal; compensation of the painted halo profiles
prevents double counting of the halo mass or electrons that are already in
the field.

\subsection{Field maps from the deterministic lattice}
\label{sec:maps-field}

Because the noise is deterministic (Section~\ref{sec:multires-noise}), field
maps are made in a separate re-paint pass that re-synthesizes each tile's
displacement fields from the same seed --- no simulation snapshot is stored.
Every lattice cell of mass $\bar\rho_m a_{\rm latt}^3$ is displaced with
equation~(\ref{eq:2lpt}) evaluated at its lightcone epoch and deposited onto
HEALPix maps \citep{Gorski2005} with kernel weights per map type:
\begin{align}
  \kappa &: \quad
  W_\kappa = \tfrac{3}{2}\Omega_m \left(\tfrac{H_0}{c}\right)^2
  (1+z)\,\chi\left(1-\tfrac{\chi}{\chi_\star}\right), \\
  \tau &: \quad
  W_\tau = \sigma_T\,\bar n_{e,0}\,f_e\,(1+z)^2 , \qquad
  {\rm kSZ}: \; -W_\tau\, v_r/c , \\
  {\rm ISW} &: \quad
  W_{\rm ISW} = \tfrac{2}{c^2}\, a H (f-1)\, \phi(\bm{q}) ,
\end{align}
where $\phi = \tfrac{3}{2}\Omega_m (H_0/c)^2 (D/a)\, \nabla^{-2}\delta_0$ is
the linear potential, $f$ the growth rate, and $f_e$ accounts for helium
ionization. \needcheck{final kernel notation pass against FieldMap.jl and
Websky eqs.\ 3.22--3.24; ISW prefactor conventions}

Cells may be subdivided ($n\le 5^3$ sub-cells) before deposition to suppress
pixelization noise, and painting runs on-device with a native ring-scheme
\texttt{ang2pix} and atomic adds at Nside 4096. Two correctness requirements
emerged from validation. First, every field map must pass an analytic
integral anchor (e.g., total deposited mass equals the geometric expectation
to machine precision; mean $\kappa$ matches its analytic value). Second, the
\emph{deposit position} matters at second order: potential-weighted kernels
(ISW) must deposit at the \emph{Lagrangian} cell position. Depositing
$\phi(\bm{q})$ at the Eulerian position imprints the density field onto the
painted potential --- a $\delta\times\phi$ cross term that exceeds the ISW
signal by up to three orders of magnitude at $\ell\gtrsim 90$. With the
Lagrangian deposit the ISW spectrum tracks the linear-theory expectation
across five decades of $C_\ell$ (Section~\ref{sec:valmaps}).

\subsection{Halo painting}
\label{sec:maps-halo}

Halo profiles are painted with the \pkg{XGPaint} package \citep{XGPaint},
to which we contributed the profiles used here:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Lensing convergence}: truncated NFW with concentration $c=7$
  (mass- and redshift-independent, the Websky choice), $\rho\propto r^{-2}$
  extension to $2\,r_{200m}$, and a compensation term --- a uniform sphere at
  mean overdensity $\Delta=3$ carrying the \emph{full painted mass}
  ($1.636\,M_{200m}$ for $c=7$) --- so each halo adds zero net mass on top of
  the full-matter field map.
  \item \textbf{Gas density / kSZ}: the \citet{Battaglia2016} AGN-feedback
  gas-density profile. Public implementations of this profile differ in the
  outer-slope convention and truncation; we implement both conventions behind
  an explicit flag (the published form, and the form used to produce the
  released Websky maps), anchor the amplitude to the $\bar\tau$ scaling
  relation of \citet{Battaglia2016} (our published-form implementation
  reproduces its $\ln\tau_0=-6.23$ anchor to 16\%), and paint kSZ with the
  per-halo line-of-sight velocity. An analogous zero-net electron
  compensation is available for combining with the full-matter momentum
  field.
  \item \textbf{tSZ}: the \citet{Battaglia2012} pressure profile, painted for
  all haloes.
  \item \textbf{CIB}: the \citet{Shang2012} halo model with the
  \citet{PlanckCIB2013} parameters, as implemented in \pkg{XGPaint} (the same
  model used by Websky).
\end{itemize}
Painted profiles are sampled at pixel centres; for cuspy profiles this loses
mass for haloes below the pixel scale, so each halo's residual (analytic
integral minus painted sum) is deposited in its central pixel, making every
halo's painted mass exact by construction.

\subsection{Composite maps}
\label{sec:maps-composite}

The released products are assembled per octant as: $\kappa$ = field
$\kappa$ + compensated NFW haloes (+ a Gaussian $z>4.5$ tail realized from
the Limber spectrum); kSZ = field momentum map + compensated gas-density
haloes above $M_{200c}>10^{13}\Msun$; tSZ and CIB are halo-only. The
compensated construction for $\kappa$ --- rather than masking haloes out of
the field --- is what matches the released Websky decomposition
(Section~\ref{sec:valmaps}).
