# Tier-A catalog statistics on the current catalogs (v3fs), with errors (2026-09-28)

**Script:** `tierA_v3fs.jl`. Job 5735978 took 29 min. Results are in `results/tierA_v3fs.txt`,
with the full log at `results/tierA_v3fs_job5735978.log`.

**Method.**
- The cap is the one inscribed in Websky's public 10°×10° patch (`halos_10x10.pksc`):
  half-angle 5.00°, Ω = 0.02395 sr.
- Shell, mass bins and windows are the same as the 2026-07 scripts (`compare_{bias,xi,pairwise_velocity,velocities}.jl`).
- The identical cap is placed at the centre of each of our **8 octants** (v3fs AM catalogs,
  with Eulerian positions and km/s velocities read directly).
- "ours" is the mean ± std over the 8 caps, i.e. the cap-to-cap sample variance. The
  Websky patch is one draw of the same geometry.
- The printed z = (W − mean)/std ignores the error of the 8-cap mean. The correct
  denominator is std·√(1 + 1/8), so **divide the printed z by 1.06**. The values below are
  corrected.
- The same pass computes the full-sky raw and AM N(>M|z) against Tinker08 M200m (plan A4).

This supersedes the single-cap, no-error Tier-A of 2026-06/07 on `oct000_finecell_AM`.

The **earlier single-cap result is confirmed with an error bar.** In 2026-07, "bias ratio
1.06 (3–15 Mpc/h)" meant ours was ~6% more biased, and the same holds here (1.068 ± 0.018)
on the current catalogs. It is significant at about 4σ in ξ. The previous single-cap b(M)
and v12 ratios (1.024…0.952; v12 0.997) are *not* reproduced: with 8 caps, ours sits 4–7%
higher in b at low mass and ~7% higher in |v12|. The old single cap was within the scatter.

Candidate causes, not investigated:
- a different mass-rank scatter at fixed AM mass;
- merge/exclusion details;
- Eulerian displacement conventions;
- tile layout.

The Fortran kept-peak test compares density only, not clustering.

Observations:
- The **raw** peak-patch mass function is within 2% of Tinker at 1e13 for z < 1.5.
- Raw exceeds Tinker for massive halos at low z: +15–35% above 1e14 at z<1, and above
  5e14 at z<0.5. Raw falls short at high z and near the floor: 0.55–0.8 at z > 2, and
  0.6–0.9 at 1e12–3e12.
- AM matches Tinker to ≤3% above 3e12, as it should.
- **Near the floor (M>1e12) AM is off by up to 17%** (z 0.5–1) and is non-monotonic in z.
  Some halos enter or leave the threshold through the table's flat extrapolation below
  Mmin = 5e11 and its bin edges. This agrees with the Websky patch (ours/W at 1.2e12 is
  ≈1.0 at z<3), so both catalogs share it.
