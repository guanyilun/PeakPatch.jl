Submitted from 1fa5b02; the catalog provenance records ee5e97e. That commit changed only job specs,
not `src/`, `ext/` or the Manifests, and every sidecar has `code.dirty = false`. v5 is v4

the field maps are regenerated.

## Maps (unweighted mean ours/Websky ratio, by ℓ range: < 300 / 300–1500 / > 1500)

All within 1–2% of v4fs. κ_lt4.5 is 1.071 / 1.098 / 1.110 (v4: 1.062 / 1.088 / 1.089).
kSZ is 1.186 / 1.231 / 1.040, and tSZ 2.951 / 1.120 / 1.020. The low-ℓ tSZ average is dominated by
a few noisy bins. CIB 143/353/857 is 1.56–1.66 at ℓ < 300, slightly lower than v4. The assembled
κ and ISW maps have no zero pixels and no seam structure.

**Against theory (full sky).** κ total/Halofit is 1.028 / 1.037 / 1.116 (v4: 1.020 / 1.027 /
1.086), so κ rises about 1–3%. That is consistent with gaussian_split removing a field error that
suppressed small-scale power (field-only κ/Halofit at ℓ > 1500: 0.571, v4 0.547). Field kSZ vs
linear theory is 0.98–1.07 for ℓ ≥ 40, within about 2% of v4 bin by bin.
