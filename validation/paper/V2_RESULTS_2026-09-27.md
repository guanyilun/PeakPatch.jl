# Production-v2 full-sky results (2026-09-27)

**Campaign:** `validation/websky_6144/production_v2/`. It uses periodic cores and splice
compensation, and bundles the deterministic merge, the data-sized merge hash and the φ_ij
Nyquist fix. **Estimators:** error model A3 (`docs/paper_comparison_plan_2026-09.md`). Full-sky
C_ℓ against the released Websky maps are in `results/auto_v2_*.txt` and the cross-spectra in
`results/cross_v2_*.txt`; the comparisons to theory are in `results/theory_v2_*.txt`. Jobs:
analysis 5703683, theory 5703908.

## Campaign

- **Jobs:** all 8 catalog+AM jobs completed in about 3 h each (the pipeline itself 122 min),
  down from about 7 h. The merge hash is sized from the data. The 8 field-map jobs took about
  1.3 h, halo painting about 10 min and CIB about 44 min.
- **Catalog size:** **198.4M halos per octant**, against 195.5M in the frozen run: +1.5%, the
  recovered seam volume. Raw N(>1.7e12) is 4.09e7, against 4.03e7. AM N(>1.7e12) is
  5.077e7 and N(>1e14) is 6.25e4, identical across octants.
- **AM bias removed:** the frozen AM had forced the full-octant Tinker counts onto a volume
  1.6% short.
- **Seams fixed:**
  - κ field and ISW have **0** exactly-zero pixels, against 190k and 1.5M. CIB is down to
    120k, the empty-pixel floor, from 1.9M.
  - Signal against distance to the octant planes now behaves like Websky's. Relative to the
    value 2–5° away, y is 1.22/1.16/1.14 (ours) against 1.18/1.16/1.11 (Websky), CIB545
    1.10/1.03 against 1.08/1.05, and κ is flat within 1%.

## Open items

1. **tSZ C_ℓ deficit, 0.82–0.88 at ℓ = 270–1400, with mean-y at 1.02.**
   - It is systematic in every octant. The per-octant C(400–800) is 6.5–7.5e-18 for ours
     against 7.7–9.0e-18 for Websky.
   - It is *not* the seam: it persists in v2.
   - C_ℓ there weighs the M ≳ 3e14, z ≲ 0.5 tail, roughly ΣM^{10/3}, whereas mean-y goes
     as ΣM^{5/3}.
   - Suspects: (a) the AM top-halo fix, which lowers the 1-halo proxy by 5–8%;
     (b) the high-mass tail of our catalog against Websky's; (c) the released map version.
     The Websky tSZ maps were replaced in 2022 with normalization and high-mass centre fixes.
   - The earlier 1.18–1.28 came from 6 caps on oct000 with an older catalog, before the
     top-halo fix. oct000 is now the highest octant (0.93), so those caps were not
     representative.
   - Next: paint `halos_10x10.pksc` through `halo_profiles.jl` and compare with
     `tsz_2048` on that patch. If it matches, the painter is exonerated. Then compare
     N(>M, z<0.5) above 1e14 per unit area, ours against the Websky patch.
2. **Field kSZ 1.15–1.20 × linear at ℓ = 70–200, the Doppler regime** (k ≈ 0.02–0.05
   h/Mpc, where the splice bump was ≲2%, so it is unchanged by compensation).
   - Candidates: ψ₂ in the velocities (linear theory omits it); shared box modes; the
     treatment of the sharp z = 4.5 cutoff.
   - Deciding test: the 3D velocity P(k) of the ICs (ψ₁ + 2ψ₂ weights) against linear
     theory, on the coarse grid.
3. **CIB normalization.** The XGPaint `CIB_Planck2013` L₀ gives a mean 1.18–1.23 × the
   released maps. Either rescale L₀ to the released mean for Websky-matched products, or
   quote the ratio.

## Update (2026-09-27): cause of the tSZ C_ℓ deficit found

1. **Painter exonerated.** Websky's own `halos_10x10.pksc`, painted by our production
   painter, was compared with the released `tsz_2048.fits`, the post-2022-fix version, on
   the same 4.5° disc (`check_tsz_painter_wsky.jl`):

   | ℓ | 204 | 330 | 534 | 837 | 1266 | 1872 | 2730 | 3666 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | C_ℓ ours / theirs | 1.21 | 1.16 | 1.11 | 1.06 | 1.04 | 1.06 | 1.13 | 1.25 |
   | cross-correlation | 1.000 | 0.999 | 0.999 | 0.999 | 0.999 | 0.999 | 0.997 | 0.992 |

   Mean y agrees to 1.006. Our painter is therefore *not* low; if anything it is 4–21%
   above their map, which is consistent with their 4 Mpc transverse table truncation.

   The release `UPDATES` file (mocks.cita.utoronto.ca/data/websky/v0.0/UPDATES) records:
   - 17-FEB-2022: "correct for ~7% error in profile normalization" (tSZ, found by B. Bolliet)
   - 08-APR-2022: "unconverged result in the center of the profile for high masses" (found
     by S. Naess)
   - 18-FEB-2022: CIB satellites inconsistent across frequencies; n-point statistics
     unaffected

   Our reference file is the post-fix version, and our port has the same `y0/2`
   normalization as the local Fortran clone, which has 2023 edits.
2. **Catalog tail truncated by the shell-search radius.**
   `nhunt = min(nbuff−1, floor(1.75·Rf_max/a))` = 15 cells = 12.8 Mpc/h with nbuff=16. The
   raw catalog header has RTHLmax = 12.83. Raw v2 oct000 has max M = 7.4e14 Msun/h, and
   AM brings that to 8.5e14. For z<1 per octant (`check_am_tail.jl`, job 5704125):

   | | ours raw | ours AM | Tinker |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | N(>1e15) | 0 | 0 | 51.5 |
   | N(>2e15) | 0 | 0 | 1.6 |
   | N(>3e14), z<0.25 | 738 | 538 | 534 |

   The raw excess at 3e14 is the pile-up just below the clamp; AM rescales it but cannot
   create the missing tail. That tail, the most massive clusters, dominates tSZ C_ℓ at
   ℓ≈300–1400. The Websky patch has 2 halos above 1e15 in 1/51 of an octant.

   The June 2026 conclusion that the clamp was harmless (INVESTIGATION_2026-06-14) held
   while the field was wrong. With the corrected field it truncates real clusters. The
   frozen and v2 campaigns, and all Tier-A/B catalog work at nbuff=16, carry this cap.
3. **Test in progress:** `production_v2/config_v3test_oct000.toml` (nbuff=25 → nhunt=24 →
   R cap 20.4 Mpc/h, M cap about 3e15). nhunt=24 is the largest the GPU shell kernel's shared
   memory allows (482 of 512 shells); the filter bank reaches 30.4 Mpc/h. Job 5704175 runs
   catalog+AM for oct000 only. Pass criterion: N(>1e15, z<1) consistent with Tinker's 51.5,
   and no pile-up at the new cap.
