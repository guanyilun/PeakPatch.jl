## Catalog

**Merge.** 200.95–201.17M pre-merge candidates per octant (v3: ~337.8M) → 112.44–112.53M
after exclusion. Volume reduction then removes 25–38 halos per octant and moves mean R_TH from
1.6525 to 1.6323 Mpc/h.

**Fewer halos below 1e12.** The 86M fewer halos per octant are almost all below 1e12, from the
per-tile threshold (pre-merge 338M → 201M; the reduction removes only 25–38 halos per octant
and shrinks mean R_TH by ~1.2%). On oct000, abundance-matched v4fs/v3fs counts are
(`ymoments_v3fs_v4fs.jl`, `results/ymoments_oct000_v3fs_v4fs.txt`):

| | 1e11–1e12 | 1e12–3e12 | ≥3e12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| N | 0.07–0.12 | 0.85–1.03 | 1.000 |

So ΣM^{5/3} (∝ mean y) drops by 3.8% (0.998 above 1e12), and the **measured full-sky mean y drops
by 4.3%** (1.035 → 0.990). Websky's catalog
keeps only halos above 10 particles before AM (~1.2–1.7e12), so v4fs mean y is closer to like
for like.
